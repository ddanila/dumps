  14 metadata results, none identified as DU-7 firmware. The broad cd-rom-dumps

  DU-7/DU7 or relevant part-number matches.

Thus no replacement for U104 has been located. Preserve the current captures
as potentially useful acquisitions, without claiming they are the first dumps.
